Privacy Policy – YeayLork Keyboard

Last updated: October 2026

YeayLork Keyboard ("the app") is a Khmer and English on-screen keyboard.

What we collect: nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no internet permission and contains no ads, analytics or third-party tracking.

What you type: text you type is processed only on your device to show word suggestions. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device: your settings (for example keyboard size, vibration) and any words you add, import or learn are saved in the app's private storage on your device so suggestions improve over time. You can clear the word library in the app's Settings, or remove all of it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Depending on your device's backup settings, Android may include this app data in your own device/Google backup.

Permissions: the app uses only the vibration permission, for optional key-press haptic feedback.

Children: the app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes: if this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
